How the counts are made

  • Every row comes from reports-4471.json. The export pages overlap, so rows are deduplicated by id first (uniqueById), then kept only when merged into #4471.
  • causes.ts places a report by its tenant: a day-first locale through the shared parser is parser; a day-first locale typing into a booking-v3 form, whose hint says MM/DD/YYYY, is hint.
  • desk-notes.json lists the reports the desk read as a one-day move. The desk counts them where they were filed, by tenant, and footnotes them; this page keeps that count and gives them a ticket of their own.
  • Nothing above is typed into this page. Change the data and the numbers change with it.

Where it stands

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October. Its engineering update names two outcomes, one per cause it knew: the parser fixed, the hint not fixed because the form is gone. The one-day move is in neither, so its ticket stays open until someone reads those five reports.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is only “one bug reported many times” when something other than the subject line says so. Count by cause, not by subject.
  • A number typed into a paragraph goes stale the day the queue moves. Let a component compute it, then write the words around it.
  • Read the footnotes. Five reports the desk already knew were different sat under the wrong cause for six months.
